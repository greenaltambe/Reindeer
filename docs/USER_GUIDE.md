# User guide

**First launch.** Six short pages: welcome, name, birth year, health conditions, meal
times, and reminder permission. Everything except the welcome and meal times can be skipped.

**Allergies and Medical ID.** On the *You* tab, add allergies (common groups or your own).
When you add a medicine with a matching ingredient, Reindeer warns you first. *Medical ID* shows
allergies, conditions and medicines on one page you can share.

**How I feel.** On *Health* or from the + button, log a symptom with how bad it is. The diary shows
which medicines you started recently and the doctor summary includes it.

**Missed a dose?** Tap an overdue dose to see what to do and a *Tell my family* button.

**Unusual readings.** After saving a blood pressure, sugar, pulse or weight reading, Reindeer may
show a note if it is very high, very low, or far from your usual. It is a guide, not a diagnosis.

**Reminders not arriving?** Open *You > App settings*. It shows whether notifications, exact alarms
and battery settings are fine, how many reminders are waiting, and has a *Test in 1 minute* button.

**Add a medicine.** Tap *Add medicine* (Today or Medicines), then search by brand or
ingredient, or type the doctor's line as written, for example `dolo 650 1-0-1 after food 5 days`; Reindeer reads the schedule and takes you straight to the review page. If it is not listed, choose *Add my own*.
Answer one question per page. The last page is a summary; tap any line to change it.

**Today.** A week calendar sits on top: tap a day (or swipe to another week) to see its
doses, grouped by time. Tap the round button to mark a dose taken, or tap the card for
Skip and Undo. Finished items move to the *Done* list. Health reminders you set also
appear here; tap one to add the reading. The gear opens app settings and *Add* adds a
medicine or a reading.

**Take a dose.** Use the buttons on the notification (Taken, Skip, Snooze 10 min) or on
the Today screen.

**Medicines.** Pause, resume, stop (keeps history), refill, or delete from the three-dot
menu. Stock and low-stock warnings appear when you entered a quantity.

**Health.** Open a reading, tap *Add reading*, enter the number. Set a daily or weekly
reminder from the reading's page. Swipe a history row to delete it. Add your height on the
You tab to see BMI. When you add a reading and no reminder is set, a switch offers
*Remind me every day*.

**Progress.** Switch between 7 and 30 days. The share icon copies a summary (medicines,
adherence, conditions, latest readings) to paste into a message for your doctor.

**You.** Your name, birth year, height, health conditions and meal times (changing meal
times moves every reminder). The gear opens **App settings**: reminder check and test
notification, theme and about.

Reindeer is a reminder tool, not medical advice.


## Searching allergies and symptoms

- **Allergies:** type a medicine, brand or ingredient; suggestions come from the built-in medicine database. Tap one to add it, or tap a common group (Penicillins, NSAIDs, Sulfa drugs...).
- **How I feel:** type a symptom in English or Hindi words (bukhar, ulti, chakkar, khansi...). If one of your medicines lists it as a side effect, Reindeer shows a note for your doctor.
- **Adding a medicine:** the review step has a collapsible "Possible side effects" card.


## Home-screen widget

Long-press the home screen, choose Widgets, then Reindeer doses. Tap Taken next to a dose to mark it done; the app records it the next time it opens. It also shows a "Running low" line when stock is short. It shows how many doses you have taken today and the next ones (late ones in red). Tap it to open the app. It updates whenever the app changes your doses.

## Backup

Settings > Backup > Save backup writes one file (choose Google Drive or phone storage). On a new phone use Restore and pick that file. Restoring replaces everything currently in the app.

## Loud reminders and your routine

- Settings > Reminders > **Loud repeating reminders**: rings at alarm volume and repeats until you tap Taken, Skip or Snooze.
- After about a week of use, the You tab may suggest moving a meal time if you consistently take doses earlier or later than planned. Tap the suggested time to update all reminders, or Not now.

## Language

Settings > Language (or the first screen of setup) switches the whole app between English, Hindi and Marathi. Reminders follow it. Names, notes and medicine names stay as you typed them. The doctor summary and Medical ID are always shared in English.

## Flexible dosing
When adding a medicine, the "Which days?" step lets you pick every day, every other day, every N days or chosen weekdays. On the amount step you can set a different amount for the other days (for example 1 tablet one day, 2 the next). You can also type "every other day" in the search line.

## Voice entry
On the Add medicine screen tap the microphone and say, for example, "Dolo 650 morning and night after food 5 days". Works in English, Hindi and Marathi (depends on the phone's speech recogniser). The first use asks for microphone permission.

## Scanning a strip or bottle
Tap the camera icon on the Add medicine screen, take or choose a photo. The text is read on the phone (nothing is uploaded) and the best medicine-name match is searched. Other lines read from the pack appear as chips to tap. Printed English text only.

## TB care (DOTS)
You tab > TB care (DOTS) > Start TB treatment. Choose the start date, weight band (suggests tablets per day), and a treatment supporter (name, optional phone, 4-digit PIN, optional Nikshay ID). Reindeer creates the standard six-month plan: 4 medicines for 2 months, then 3 medicines for 4 months, once a day on an empty stomach, with daily reminders.
- Each dose can be confirmed by the supporter typing their PIN (the dose is marked taken and "Watched by").
- Today's list shows a TB banner with the day number (of 168) and whether today's dose is confirmed.
- After a missed dose, one tap sends the supporter a message; after 2 missed days in a row a warning tells the patient to contact the TB centre.
- The screen also shows progress, adherence and how many doses were watched, usual check-up dates, safety notes, and a report to share with the health worker.
- Data stays on the phone and is included in backups.
